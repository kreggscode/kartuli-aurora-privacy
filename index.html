Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Developer: Kregg (“we”, “us”, “our”)

App: Georgian Alphabets (Kartuli Aurora) · Package: com.kreggscode.georgianalphabets

Kartuli Aurora teaches the Georgian alphabet, words, phrases and grammar. It does not require an account, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and why.

What stays on your device

  • your name (only if you enter one), daily goal, XP, streak, lesson and word progress;
  • achievements and sound/haptics preferences;
  • the current AI conversation while the chat screen is open.

Progress is stored locally in the app’s private storage. We do not operate a server and we do not receive or keep a copy of it. Resetting progress in Settings, clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app removes it.

What leaves your device

Ask Aurora (AI tutor)

When you send a message in Ask Aurora, the recent messages of that conversation and your question are transmitted over HTTPS to Pollinations (pollinations.ai, endpoint text.pollinations.ai), a third-party AI service, so that a reply can be generated. Pollinations processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store those messages.

Please do not share sensitive personal information — such as medical, financial or identity details — in the AI chat. The tutor is optional; every other feature works without an internet connection and without sending any data.

Reporting an AI answer

Every AI answer has a Report action. It opens your own email app with the question and answer prefilled and addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. The same contact is available in Settings → Support.

Audio

Lessons, letters, words and phrases use recordings bundled inside the app, so they play offline. If a recording is missing, the app may ask your device’s text-to-speech engine to read the text; that engine is provided by your device or its TTS vendor, not by us.

What we do not collect

  • no name, email or phone number is required or collected by us;
  • no location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, calendar or files access;
  • no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • no payment data — the app is distributed through Google Play.

Children

Kartuli Aurora is a general-audience educational app intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and the AI tutor is not designed for children.

Security and retention

Requests to the AI tutor use HTTPS. We retain no personal data because we receive none. Local progress remains until you reset it, clear app data or uninstall the app. Chat messages are kept in memory while the screen is open and are not stored by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing.
